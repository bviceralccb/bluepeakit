Accessibility

Website Accessibility

BluePeak Managed IT LLC wants everyone to be able to use this website. We work to make its content and features accessible and welcome feedback about barriers you encounter.

Using this site

You can navigate the site with a keyboard, use the skip link to move to the main content, and enlarge text with your browser’s zoom controls. The contact form opens your email application so you can review and send your request.

Report an accessibility issue

If you have trouble using any part of this site or need its information in another format, email bluepeak.managed@gmail.com or call (626) 617-9234. Please include the page and a description of the problem. Details about your browser or assistive technology are helpful if you wish to share them.

We will review your feedback and work with you to provide the information you need.
